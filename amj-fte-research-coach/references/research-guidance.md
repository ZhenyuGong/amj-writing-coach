# Evidence-bound guidance from the read subset

Use the linked [paper notes](paper-notes.md) for nuances and page anchors. The points below are synthesized decisions, not journal rules.

## Starting a project

- For a phenomenon-driven project, specify what is neglected, poorly understood, or puzzling before choosing a contribution route. The 2026 editorial describes builder, expander, and tester routes; identify the reader and prior knowledge each route would change. Early openness to explanations does not justify presenting post hoc quantitative hypotheses as prespecified. [Ter Wal et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.5465/amj.2026.4003)
- For a genuinely deductive quantitative study, check whether construct definitions, measures, design, and theoretical mechanism were aligned before results were known. If an insight emerged later, label it exploratory or design follow-up tests. [Shaw, 2017](https://doi.org/10.5465/amj.2017.4003)
- Map nine connected elements: puzzle; audience/prior research; question; constructs and relationships; setting; design/analysis; findings; contribution; boundaries. Repair mismatches before line editing. The canvas is a flexible planning aid, not a required manuscript template. [Dorobantu et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.5465/amj.2024.4005)

## Making an explanatory contribution

- In qualitative work, an unusual setting, pattern, sequence, or diagram alone is an empirical finding. Explain why it occurs, what tension or mechanism it reveals, which concepts travel beyond the setting, and how this changes a relevant conversation. Name connections in a process model instead of relying on arrows. [Rouse et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.5465/amj.2025.4002)
- In multimethod work, state what each method uniquely resolves and how the studies integrate. Match inductive or deductive methods to the maturity of theory. A second method needs a theoretical or inferential job beyond adding volume; plan the cross-study explanation. [Wellman et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.5465/amj.2023.4004)
- For an introduction, make the consequence of the puzzle legible, identify its audience, then show how the study changes the conversation. [Grant & Pollock, 2011](https://doi.org/10.5465/amj.2011.4000)

## Research practice

- For a collaboration, align motivation, work style, contribution, authorship expectations, milestones, data access, and communication early; revisit assignments as the project evolves. The source is a survey and editorial reflection, not a causal estimate of what guarantees success. [Ravasi et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.5465/amj.2024.4003)
- Use generative AI as a possible aid across synthesis, development, evaluation, and translation, with human verification of sources, analyses, data provenance, and interpretations. The 2023 editorial identifies opportunities and risks but is not a current AI-use policy. [Grimes et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.5465/amj.2023.4006)
