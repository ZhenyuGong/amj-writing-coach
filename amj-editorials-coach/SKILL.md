# AMJ From the Editors research coach

This skill draws on the broader [official AMJ From the Editors collection](references/collection.md). It is independent of `amj-writing-coach`, which remains dedicated to the seven-part *Publishing in AMJ* writing series. Do not silently merge their guidance or replace the user's own prose with an editorial house style.

## Use the evidence selectively

1. Identify the user's actual problem, manuscript type, available evidence, and requested output. Preserve their chosen argument and voice unless they ask to change them.
2. Read [reading-status.md](references/reading-status.md) before invoking an editorial. Use the [paper notes](references/paper-notes.md) only for articles with a locally accessed full text and substantive reading notes. A title in the [collection inventory](references/collection.md) is a discovery lead, not evidence of an article's specific advice.
3. Choose one or two relevant lenses from [research-guidance.md](references/research-guidance.md). Diagnose the user's draft concretely; distinguish the editorial's claim from your own inference.
4. Give a revision, research decision, or short prioritized plan. Cite the relevant DOI and say when advice comes from a historical editorial rather than a current journal requirement.

## Routing

- Phenomenon-driven question and contribution: 2026 phenomenon editorial; for hypothesis-driven quantitative work, also consider the 2017 theory-first editorial.
- Qualitative contribution and explanation: 2025 qualitative editorial.
- Overall research coherence: 2024 management research canvas.
- Multimethod study architecture: 2023 multimethod editorial.
- Team process and coauthorship: 2024 collaboration editorial.
- Generative AI in scholarship: 2023 AI editorial; check current AMJ and institution policies separately.
- Introduction hook: the 2011 *Setting the Hook* editorial, shared with the seven-part series.

When a collection item remains unread, locate the source or ask the user for its PDF before offering article-specific guidance. Do not present the current subset as a synthesis of all 35 articles. Keep downloaded PDFs in the local private library, outside this public skill.
